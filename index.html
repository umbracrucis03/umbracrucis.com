The Whispering Clock

It was always three minutes fast. Every morning, I would correct it. Every evening, it had crept ahead again, ticking inexorably faster than the world outside. The store owner promised me it was an heirloom. My grandmother told me to burn it.

I should have listened. Last night, at 2:57 AM, I heard it. A whisper, not a tick. My name. The seconds have stopped moving. The minutes have stopped moving. And outside, the sun won't rise...
